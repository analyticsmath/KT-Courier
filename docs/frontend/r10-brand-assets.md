# R10 brand assets

## Authority and digital-mark limitation

The existing readable, type-based `KT Couriers` wordmark remains the primary public identity through `components/public-v2/brand/KtCouriersWordmark.tsx`. R10 adds a compact **KT** digital mark for browser and social utility use. It is not presented as a registered trademark, a final corporate filing, a certification, or a replacement for a future approved brand identity.

The mark uses carbon `#101210`, white `#FFFFFF`, and one oxide checkpoint `#D83A2E`. Use carbon on light surfaces or white lettering on a carbon field. Keep at least one checkpoint diameter of clear space. Do not place it on busy media, stretch it, add shadows, gradients, borders, badges, trucks, shields, wings, pins, or additional taglines.

The wordmark remains readable at all public-header sizes. The compact mark must remain legible at 16px and 32px, recognizable at 48px, and may never contain type beyond “KT”. The generated icon route is designed for 192px/512px manifest output; the Apple route is 180px.

## Open Graph and manifest policy

The default social card is deliberately graphic and text-led: no stock photograph, price, review, product claim, dashboard image, or legal screenshot is used. R10 replaced route-level social references to provisional campaign media with the default brand card. The manifest is browser metadata only; it does not introduce a service worker, offline behavior, background sync, push support, or standalone PWA claim.
