# R17 — Promoter Illustrations

R17 creates no new illustration. It reuses the R13 data-free SVG grammar and keeps illustrations out of active financial tables and forms.

| Component | R17 state | Concept and semantics | Desktop/mobile use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `RouteQueueIllustration` | No attributed referrals, no referral code, reporting unavailable, account unavailable | Restrained operational route geometry; decorative by default | Compact empty/unavailable state, above explanatory text |
| `SecureLedgerIllustration` | No earning records, no withdrawal requests, wallet not provisioned | Verified ledger record; decorative by default | Compact financial empty/unavailable state, never a balance background |

Both inherit the R13 palette: carbon, cool mineral neutral, oxide, and operational teal. They contain no text, gradients, purple, ivory, glossy 3D treatment, money imagery, leaderboard/trophy treatment, social logos, customer data, chart data, or hidden business information. There is no dominant promoter illustration on active data screens.
