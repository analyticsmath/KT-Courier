# Catalog compliance

Product types may require ingredients, allergens, nutrition, net quantity, origin, shelf-life, age restriction, condition/refurbishment disclosure, hazardous warnings, warranty, manufacturer/importer, and mandatory labels.

Initial policy fails publication closed for weapons, ammunition, illegal goods, controlled substances, prescription medicines, tobacco/nicotine, alcohol pending authorization, counterfeit/unsafe goods, and recalls. Refurbished/reconditioned products require condition disclosure. The policy never supplies checkout logic for restricted items.

This is implementation policy, not production legal certification. Legal/category decisions and live restriction evidence require architect/legal review in Phase 26.5.
