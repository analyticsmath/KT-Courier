# Catalog modifiers

Modifier groups are reusable inside one store and attach to offers. Each group has min/max selection rules, required state, display order, and flat options. A required group must require at least one selection and minimum cannot exceed maximum.

Option deltas are exact zero-or-positive ZAR amounts. Negative discounts, recursive groups, and cart calculation are deferred. Variants remain exact product configurations; modifiers remain customer-selectable offer additions.
