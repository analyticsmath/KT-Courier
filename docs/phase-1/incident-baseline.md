|---|---|---|---|---|
| `P1-INC-01` | Guest parcel quote succeeds (`R129 · Standard`), but signed-in parcel booking fails to reach Card payment or appears not to proceed. | Parcel booking navigation (`components/forms/PublicDeliveryQuoteForm.tsx`, `lib/client-platform/parcel-booking-navigation.ts`), `/quote` principal prefill (`app/(public)/quote/page.tsx`), and payment preparation (`app/api/orders/[id]/payment/route.ts`, `lib/services/payment-preparation.service.ts`) | 1. Payment preparation fails closed with `PAYMENT_POLICY_NOT_CONFIGURED` or `PAYSTACK_NOT_CONFIGURED` if active `PaymentMethodPolicy` (`DIGITAL_ONLY`) or `PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY` is missing in the target deployment. | **Resolved in code; fail-closed config gates preserved.** `parcelBookingDestination()` routes `DIGITAL_ONLY` bookings directly to `/orders/:orderNumber/payment` (with `?workspace=store` for store principals). `/quote` now loads customer `savedAddresses` and store `defaultPickup`, and `QuoteAddressSchema` + `PublicDeliveryQuoteForm` preserve `line2`. Actionable HTTP status/error codes (`PAYMENT_POLICY_NOT_CONFIGURED`, `PAYSTACK_NOT_CONFIGURED`, `PAYMENT_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`) are surfaced cleanly. |
| `P1-INC-02` | Customer signs in for parcel booking, then cannot seamlessly browse/cart/checkout in Shop (`STORE` workspace cookie or route guard friction). | Session & workspace principal resolution (`lib/auth/session.ts`, `lib/auth/workspace.ts`, `app/(public)/shop/**`, `app/(public)/cart/page.tsx`, `app/(public)/checkout/[reference]/page.tsx`) | Public storefront (`/shop`), cart (`/cart`, `/api/cart/**`), and marketplace checkout (`/checkout/[reference]`, `/api/checkout/**`) resolve the authenticated `CUSTOMER` session directly and merge guest carts via `POST /api/cart/merge`. | `P1-INC-03` | Customer saved default delivery address and account contact details are not prefilled on marketplace checkout. | Marketplace checkout read projection (`app/api/checkout/[reference]/route.ts`, `lib/marketplace-checkout/customer-checkout-prefill.ts`, `components/public-v2/commerce/CheckoutExperience.tsx`) | Checkout previously required manual re-entry of contact name/email/phone and delivery address even when the signed-in customer had verified account contact details and a default saved `Address` (`type: CUSTOMER` or `DROPOFF`). | **Resolved and verified.** `GET /api/checkout/[reference]` returns `accountContact` and `savedAddresses` (prioritized by `selectableCustomerAddresses()`), and `CheckoutExperience.tsx` prefills the contact fields and default saved delivery address while still re-verifying geocoding and route coverage on submit (`P1-A11`). |
| `P1-INC-04` | Store collection address is not prefilled when a store books a parcel, or store pickup fails marketplace checkout (`STORE_PICKUP_UNAVAILABLE`). | Store pickup selection & parcel quote prefill (`lib/services/store-addresses.service.ts`, `lib/marketplace-checkout/store-pickup-selection.ts`, `app/(store)/store/new-delivery/page.tsx`, `app/(public)/quote/page.tsx`) | 1. Store parcel booking on `/store/new-delivery` and `/quote` requires `getStorePickupAddress(user.id, "deliveries")` passed as `defaultPickup`.<br>2. Marketplace checkout requires a store-owned `Address` (`type: PICKUP`, `storeId = store.id`) with street `line1`, `city`, `province`, `postalCode`, `placeId`, and valid South African `latitude`/`longitude` (`completeStorePickupAddress()`). Legacy flat `Store.addressLine1` without verified coordinates cannot authorize road-route pricing. | **Resolved in code; fail-closed coordinate gate preserved.** Both `/store/new-delivery` and `/quote` prefill `defaultPickup` for `STORE` principals. Admin/vendor readiness diagnostics (`lib/admin/store-pickup-readiness.ts`) identify exact missing pickup fields without fabricating coordinates (`P1-A21`, `P1-A22`). |
| `P1-INC-05` | Marketplace checkout fails with `"Delivery pricing is not available for the selected store items"` (`DELIVERY_PRICING_UNAVAILABLE`). | Marketplace checkout pricing & packing (`lib/marketplace-checkout/checkout.service.ts`, `lib/marketplace-checkout/store-item-packing.ts`, `lib/commercial/parcel-profiles.ts`) | Triggered when: (a) store items lack physical weight/dimension metadata or exceed active `SMALL`/`MEDIUM`/`LARGE` `ParcelProfileVersion` bounds; (b) no active `DeliveryServiceDefinition` (`CLIENT_ECONOMY`, `CLIENT_STANDARD`, `CLIENT_EXPRESS`) covers the store pickup and customer dropoff regions; or (c) Google Routes / Geocoding cannot verify the route. | **Resolved and verified.** `packStoreOrderItems()` deterministically packs multi-item store baskets into `SMALL`/`MEDIUM`/`LARGE` profiles, and checkout returns specific remediation codes rather than opaque failures (`P1-A13`, `P1-A23`). |
| `P1-INC-06` | Marketplace checkout fails with `"One or more stores are missing an approved operational policy"` (`STORE_ORDER_POLICY_UNAVAILABLE`). | Store operational policy gate (`lib/store-orders/store-order-policy.ts`, `lib/marketplace-checkout/checkout.service.ts`, `app/api/admin/stores/[id]/operational-policy/route.ts`) | Intentional fail-closed commercial invariant: every store in a marketplace checkout must have an active `StoreOrderOperationalPolicy` (`status: ACTIVE`, `effectiveFrom <= now`, `acceptanceWindowMinutes`, `preparationSlaMinutes`, `substitutionPolicy`, `partialFulfilmentAllowed`) approved by an admin distinct from the store owner (`approvedByUserId !== store.ownerUserId`). | **Preserved fail-closed gate with admin/store remediation UI.** Store readiness checklists and `/admin/stores/[id]` surface missing `StoreOrderOperationalPolicy` records and enforce maker-checker separation (`P1-A19`, `P1-A20`). |
| `P1-INC-07` | Store seller legal identity form rejects `"Pammy Bakes"` as `identityVersion` and allows confusion where shop name `"PammyBakes"` is typed into `sourceEvidenceReference`. | Seller identity validation, service & UI (`lib/validation/store-seller-identity.ts`, `lib/services/store-seller-identity.service.ts`, `app/api/store/seller-identity/route.ts`, `components/store/StoreSellerIdentityForm.tsx`, `lib/client-platform/api.ts`) | 1. `identityVersion` requires an identifier without spaces (`^[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9_.-]*$`), which confused owners entering their trading name.<br>2. `submitStoreSellerIdentity()` only checked `privateMediaObject` when `sourceEvidenceReference.startsWith("PMO-")`, allowing arbitrary strings like `"PammyBakes"` to pass validation without an uploaded verification document. | **Resolved and verified.** `StoreSellerIdentityForm` now auto-suggests `PAMMY_BAKES_V1` from `tradingName` / `legalName` via `suggestSellerIdentityVersion()`, marks `sourceEvidenceReference` read-only (populated from `/api/store/seller-identity/documents`), returns field-level `fieldErrors` on 422, and `assertCanonicalSellerEvidenceReference()` + `submitStoreSellerIdentity()` reject arbitrary strings like `"PammyBakes"` with HTTP 422 (`SELLER_EVIDENCE_INVALID`) (`P1-A16`, `P1-A17`). |
| `P1-INC-08` | Stores appear Live or hidden inconsistently without clear certification state. | Storefront publication & checkout certification (`lib/storefront/store-publication-readiness.ts`, `lib/admin/store-checkout-certification.service.ts`, `components/admin/StoreCheckoutCertificationPanel.tsx`) | A store must not appear publicly `Live` based solely on `Store.status === "ACTIVE"`.
