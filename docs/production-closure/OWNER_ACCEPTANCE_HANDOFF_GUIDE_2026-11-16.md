   - Do **NOT** flip `CHECKOUT_PUBLIC_ENABLED` to public live mode until all acceptance checks (`AC-01`..`AC-11`, `L-01`..`L-04`) pass and independent legal/finance sign-offs exist.
3. **Delivery Regions & Tariffs (`L-02` Audit Note):**
   - Production exposes 7 active `DeliveryRegion` records (`gauteng-province-wide` with `coverageMode: "PROVINCE"`, plus 6 legacy `RADIUS` regions with `boundaryIssues: ["centerLat","centerLng","coverageRadiusKm","maxDistanceKm"]`).

   - Owner must decide in `/admin/regions` & `/admin/delivery-configuration` whether `gauteng-province-wide` remains authorized or whether active services should restrict `regionIds` explicitly to populated Johannesburg & Pretoria zones.

## 2. Controlled Test Persona Setup (Owner-Supplied Accounts)

Create separate owner-controlled test accounts using real owner mailboxes (never invent placeholder mailboxes or reuse one person as both maker and checker):
- **`AC-01` Customer** & **Second Foreign Customer** (`CUSTOMER`): Exercises parcel booking, marketplace shopping, saved addresses, wallet, and BOLA denial.
- **`AC-02` Returning Customer** (`CUSTOMER`): Pre-seeded repeat delivery/checkout address history, legacy rows missing suburb/map pin, loyalty & referrals.
- **`AC-03` / `AC-04` Store Owners** (`STORE`): Includes an unregistered sole-trader persona (e.g., owner legal name + `Pammy Bakes` trading name + genuine test verification document).
- **`AC-05` Finance** & **`AC-06` Operations** (`ADMIN` / authorized operators): Separate financial approval vs dispatch/coverage/notification administration.
- **`AC-07` / `AC-08` Drivers** (`DRIVER`): Controlled driver accounts with valid test docs, vehicle record, and bank remittance proof / cash custody limit (e.g., R1,000).
- **`AC-09` / `AC-10` Maker & Independent Checker Admins** (`ADMIN` + `SUPER_ADMIN`): Distinct accounts for two-person approvals (seller identity, store readiness/go-live, commissions, refunds, notifications).
- **`AC-11` Promoter** (*only if separately approved*): Intentionally gated off at launch (`PROMOTERS_PRODUCTION_VALIDATION_APPROVED = false`).
