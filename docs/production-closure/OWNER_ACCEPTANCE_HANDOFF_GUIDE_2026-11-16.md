Before beginning persona testing, verify these non-secret settings with an authorized infrastructure / `SUPER_ADMIN` operator:

   - Confirm non-sensitive health checks return `200`: `GET /api/health` and `GET /api/ready` (`database: "reachable"`, `redis.status: "HEALTHY"`).
2. **Railway Worker & Environment Alignment (`L-04` Audit Note)**:
   - Inspection at `f913616803818e8e92b12e983dbed9c0157050ea` observed both `operations` and `query-storefront-categories` executing `scripts/production-processors.ts`, while `operations` lacked `GOOGLE_MAPS_SERVER_KEY` and `KT_OWNER_*` variables present on `web` and `query-storefront-categories`. Reconcile worker service ownership and variable parity (and keep `admin-replacement-inspection` off / discarded selectively only after inspecting the exact diff per `OPERATOR_ACTIONS_REQUIRED.md`).
   - Do **NOT** flip `CHECKOUT_PUBLIC_ENABLED` to public live mode until all acceptance checks (`AC-01`..`AC-11`, `L-01`..`L-04`) pass and independent legal/finance sign-offs exist.
3. **Delivery Regions & Tariffs (`L-02` Audit Note)**:
   - Production currently exposes 7 active `DeliveryRegion` records (`gauteng-province-wide` with `coverageMode: "PROVINCE"`, plus 6 legacy `RADIUS` regions with `boundaryIssues: ["centerLat","centerLng","coverageRadiusKm","maxDistanceKm"]`).

   - High-risk locality rules (`delivery.high_risk_areas`) contain the 42 seeded Johannesburg and Pretoria/Tshwane areas with cash disabled conservatively when pickup or dropoff matches a high-risk or ambiguous district.
   - Verify whether you intend `gauteng-province-wide` to stay active or restrict service `regionIds` explicitly to populated Johannesburg & Pretoria zones in `/admin/regions` & `/admin/settings`.

## 2. Controlled Test Persona Setup (Owner-Supplied Accounts)

Create or designate separate test accounts using real owner-controlled mailboxes (never invent fake emails or reuse the same user as both maker and independent reviewer):
1. **Owner Customer (`CUSTOMER`)**: Used across parcel quotes/bookings (`/quote`, `/account/addresses`) AND marketplace cart/checkout (`/shop`, `/cart`, `/checkout`).
2. **Foreign Customer (`CUSTOMER`)**: Second unrelated account to prove ownership/BOLA isolation (`401`/`403`/`404`).
3. **Owner Sole-Trader / Test Store (`STORE`)**: E.g. testing unregistered sole trader (`Pammy Bakes` trading name) with real test verification PDF/image.
4. **Owner Test Driver (`DRIVER`)**: Equipped with valid test documents and assigned bank remittance / cash limit (e.g. R1,000).
5. **Maker Operator (`ADMIN`)** & **Independent Reviewer / Switch Operator (`SUPER_ADMIN`)**: Distinct accounts required for two-person maker/checker approvals.
