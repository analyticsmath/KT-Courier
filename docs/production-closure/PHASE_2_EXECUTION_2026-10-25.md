## Additional Phase 2 cart authorization and concurrent-edit hardening

An independent policy audit found three fail-open paths in the cart service, reproduced first as failing tests: (1) quantity revalidation could silently substitute a different store/SKU or quantity; (2) a guest cart changed after a user's claim screen had been displayed could be claimed using a stale version; (3) a checkout-locked guest cart could be merged into a customer cart despite its lifecycle lock. `lib/marketplace-checkout/cart-mutation.service.ts` now validates the revalidated SKU fingerprint/store/quantity and uses the guest's expected optimistic version and ACTIVE lifecycle for claims and merges. All invalid paths reject before persistence and before writing operation receipts. Original quantity/cash/security behaviour is unchanged. ## Phase 2 identity and signup failure semantics

A targeted signup audit found that all transaction failures—including expired, used or concurrently claimed vendor onboarding links—were previously reported as `ACCOUNT_EMAIL_UNAVAILABLE`, despite rolling back the account before any verification email could be sent. Signup now distinguishes `STORE_INVITATION_INVALID` (HTTP 409 with a safe replacement-link instruction) from unexpected account-creation failures (HTTP 503 `ACCOUNT_CREATION_UNAVAILABLE` with no database or email-provider claims). An actual security-email readiness failure remains separately governed before signup starts. Concurrent invitation claims throw the typed refusal inside the same transaction. Regression: `tests/auth/signup-invitation-error.test.ts`, `tests/admin/store-invite-policy.test.ts`.

## Trusted multi-item consignment delivery classification (new Phase 2 engineering)

Previously all marketplace checkout snapshots with more than one unit or offer line returned `AGGREGATE_PACKING_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`; code could not classify measured packages that legitimately contained multiple products. The existing safety boundary (no guessed weights, no shopper-supplied size, no unreviewed size-specific tariff) remains.

`TrustedConsignmentSchema` now accepts exact store, offer, variant, current publication revision, modifier selections **and quantities for every line in a physically measured, sealed consignment**. The classified dimensions and total weight must be real consignment measurements. They are not computed from individual products. The rule lives in the existing auditable versioned `SystemSetting` approval system; the author cannot self-review and `APPROVED` is not `ACTIVE`. The latest active version is authoritative, and a missing, ambiguous, changed, expired, oversized or unapproved consignment still cannot obtain a size-specific quote. The existing approved, explicit `ANY` tariff fallback remains unchanged. Single-item legacy versions remain readable. New admin authoring UX is in `MarketplaceTrustedPackageManager` / `MeasuredConsignmentDraft`, and preparing a vendor-measured single-item review preserves any active reviewed consignments rather than silently discarding them.

**Evidence:** RED-before-GREEN `tests/marketplace-checkout/parcel-classification.test.ts`, strict-schema and negative selection/approval cases; admin-role SSR and labels `tests/admin/marketplace-consignment-authoring.test.ts`. Real PostgreSQL scenario added to `tests/integration/marketplace-checkout-courier-tariffs.integration.test.ts`: 2-unit checkout cannot quote before approved sealed measurements, independently reviewed/activated bundle produces a frozen tariff quote, changing customer quantity refuses pricing again without inserting an additional quote. First PostgreSQL execution on `d8f55420b304f9d646d1efdd1959fb64f8563d02` proved the approved **MEDIUM** sealed package actually selects the **R129.00 MEDIUM tariff**, while the new assertion had incorrectly demanded R89.00 (the SMALL rate). This is a test expectation mismatch, not a pricing defect. The new exact-commit rerun must prove the corrected R129.00 frozen quote and quantity-change refusal before closure. Product/vendor physical measurements and actual client-approved packaging policy remain external, and **no live policy or product row is activated by this release**. Until that authority and a genuine operational proof exist, `marketplace_parcel_classification` remains OPEN and the terminal launch gate remains NOT_READY.
