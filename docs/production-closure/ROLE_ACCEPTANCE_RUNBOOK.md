# Customer, store, driver and admin acceptance runbook

Run this only in an authorized test environment with disposable records and provider-sanctioned test mode. Record the exact application commit, environment, date, tester role, case outcome, safe reference IDs and links to access-controlled evidence. Do not record passwords, OTP values, private documents, customer contact details, card details or provider keys. A test-mode result does not certify a live charge or physical delivery.

Before starting, verify that the test environment has an approved test service area and route, active tariffs and appropriate sandbox payment policy, a test merchant with a real reviewed pickup fixture and identity, an independently approved driver and vehicle, an email delivery mechanism, and separate authorized operator and reviewer accounts. Use only data that the environment owner authorized for testing. Confirm the application and worker are running the same commit. Do not change production policies or insert financial success to satisfy a test.

| Role | Positive case | Negative and recovery case | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer | Obtain a guest parcel quote, sign in or register and verify email, book with the same account, and follow the payment handoff. Check pending status until a verified test-provider event and journal exist. | Unsupported region, imprecise address, disabled service, expired quote, duplicate booking and retry after an interrupted response must not create a second order or false payment. | Quote and order references, status history, test payment and journal references. |
| Customer | Move between parcel account and shop; use a saved address, add products, review delivery fees, complete an authorized test-mode checkout and inspect the resulting orders. | A different customer cannot read or alter the checkout; a missing store pickup, unavailable route or provider outage must show a useful error and prevent payment. | Checkout version, store group, quote and payment references; redacted screenshots. |
| Store | Register or sign in, supply legal owner and trading details, upload genuine test evidence, set pickup and hours, and request independent identity and checkout review. List permitted products and complete an authorized test purchase. | Unregistered individuals may omit registration and VAT references; invalid identity versions need clear errors. A changed pickup invalidates certification; another store and the preparer cannot approve their own evidence. | Review decisions, certificate revision, product and test-order references; no evidence-file content. |
| Store | Accept and prepare a paid marketplace order, hand it to the assigned driver, and separately quote and book a parcel from the store's verified pickup. | Unpaid orders cannot be fulfilled, another store's pickup cannot be used, and an unavailable product cannot be silently substituted or oversold. | Store order, shipment, payment and handoff references and role-scoped status history. |
| Driver | Complete onboarding with approved test documents and vehicle, go online, accept an eligible offer, perform sender handoff and recipient delivery proof, then inspect earnings and any cash obligations. | Unapproved or expired documents, wrong actor, wrong/expired challenge, duplicate proof, and a cash obligation over capacity must not advance custody. Record outcomes without capturing challenge values. | Assignment, custody, proof, earning and cash-obligation references, with safe timestamps. |
| Admin and finance | Review store and driver independently; manage test coverage, service and pricing; inspect notifications, payments, orders, ledger, and reconciliation. Exercise an authorized test refund or cash-deposit review with separate maker and checker. | Self-approval, foreign-object access, high-risk COD, unverified payment, duplicate event and unverified bank proof must fail without financial or fulfilment effects. | Approval revisions, audit entries, test journal and reconciliation references, with roles and timestamps. |
| Recovery | Interrupt a provider or network response and reopen the existing booking or checkout. Reprocess an authorized duplicate event and restart a disposable worker. | An unknown provider outcome must stay unresolved until verified; a retry must not issue a second charge, journal, fulfilment or reward. | Original and replay operation references, final state and journal counts. |

Record each result as PASS, FAIL or BLOCKED, with a reason and a link to restricted evidence. A FAIL remains an engineering issue; a BLOCKED case identifies the missing real configuration, provider authority, legal/commercial decision or field participant. Repeat affected cases after any fix. Production launch additionally requires exact-commit CI and database/browser evidence, authorized live-provider and operational acceptance, current genuine records, and independent owner/legal/financial sign-off. Keep launch gates unchanged until those requirements are actually met.
