## 1. Source Precedence Order (All 9 Documents + Client Rulings Cumulative)

Where two documents conflict on the same behavior, controlling precedence follows:
1. **Latest client Q&A and explicitly reported functional defects** (highest priority; incl. Nov 5 WhatsApp video, sole-trader `PAMMY_BAKES_V1` form, unified customer account, Standard = 1–2 business days, referral/loyalty/first-store rules).
2. **KT Couriers – Website Developer Brief** (`§§3.1–3.15`, `§11` acceptance plan).
3. **KT COURIERS — WEBSITE & SYSTEM CHANGES** and **KT COURIERS — Delivery and Access** (equal precedence).
4. **Remaining supplied client documents** (`SHIPPING & DELIVERY`, `ABOUT US`, `TERMS AND CONDITIONS`, `PRIVACY POLICY`, `KT_Couriers_Updated Details`, `REFUND & CANCELLATION`).

Mandatory South African tax, privacy/POPIA, identity, and financial integrity guardrails never bend to convenience overrides.
