## 1. Executive Summary of Remediated Findings (`W1`–`W4`)

This targeted closure closes the four independent audit gaps identified on baseline `313be5e73dd8d6b34362df19e96851dcedb8f906` without rewriting the core Paystack, wallet, double-entry ledger, or fulfillment state machines:

1. **W1 — Admin Alert Retry Reauthorization, BOLA & Resend Delivery Truthfulness (`Sec 2` & `Sec 3C`):**
   - `retryPendingAdminAlertDeliveries` and initial dispatch in [`lib/notifications/admin-alert-dispatch.service.ts`](../../lib/notifications/admin-alert-dispatch.service.ts) now call `resolveRecipientCurrentEligibility` immediately before every `provider.send()` invocation, re-checking `notifications.admin_alert_recipients` (active flag, email deletion, event subscription, `roleScope`) **and** live `User` DB rows (`status === "ACTIVE"` and `role in ["ADMIN", "SUPER_ADMIN"]` when an account exists for that email).
   - Revoked, disabled, deleted, unsubscribed, or scope-mismatched recipients transition deterministically to `deliveryStatus: "CANCELLED_BY_POLICY"` (`eligibilityStatus: "ELIGIBILITY_BLOCKED"`), fail `EmailLog`, and record a durable `NotificationAuditEvent` (`ADMIN_ALERT_DELIVERY_CANCELLED_BY_POLICY`) with **zero** provider calls. Concurrent policy changes during an in-flight provider HTTP call are explicitly recorded via `ADMIN_ALERT_POLICY_CHANGED_IN_FLIGHT`.
   - [`lib/services/admin-settings.service.ts`](../../lib/services/admin-settings.service.ts) enforces `PERMISSIONS.SETTINGS_UPDATE` (`requireAdminApiPermission` BOLA check) and `SUPER_ADMIN` role authority before modifying `ALL` / `SUPER_ADMIN` entries in `notifications.admin_alert_recipients`.
   - API acceptance is truthfully surfaced as `PROVIDER_ACCEPTED` (`inboxDeliveryStatus: "UNKNOWN_PENDING_PROVIDER_RECEIPT"`) with `providerMessageReference`, separated cleanly from webhook-verified `DELIVERED`, `BOUNCED`, and `COMPLAINED` states in [`components/admin/AdminAlertsConsole.tsx`](../../components/admin/AdminAlertsConsole.tsx).
   - Added [`app/api/webhooks/resend/route.ts`](../../app/api/webhooks/resend/route.ts) and [`lib/notifications/resend-webhook.service.ts`](../../lib/notifications/resend-webhook.service.ts) verifying Svix HMAC-SHA256 headers (`svix-id`, `svix-timestamp` within `300s`, `svix-signature` against `RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET`), enforcing idempotent replay detection and rate limiting (`300/min`), and updating both customer `NotificationDelivery` and `ADMIN_ALERT` `NotificationEventIntent` rows via `applyResendWebhookEventToAdminAlerts`.

2. **W2 — Accurate 50/50 `DEPOSIT_PLUS_COD`, `FULL_COD` & `WALLET_ONLY` Parcel Notifications (`Sec 3B`):**
   - Added [`lib/services/courier-payment-split-summary.ts`](../../lib/services/courier-payment-split-summary.ts) (`describeCourierOrderPaymentSplitFromDb`) computing exact canonical amounts strictly from persisted `Order`, `Payment`, `CashOnDelivery`, and `LedgerJournal` rows (`totalZar`, `depositPaidZar`, `walletPaidZar`, `providerPaidZar`, `cashDueZar`, including odd-cent splits and wallet+Paystack hybrid deposits).
   - Updated customer templates & payloads ([`lib/notifications/required-domain-definitions.ts`](../../lib/notifications/required-domain-definitions.ts), [`lib/notifications/required-domain-configuration.ts`](../../lib/notifications/required-domain-configuration.ts), [`lib/notifications/required-domain-payload.ts`](../../lib/notifications/required-domain-payload.ts)) and admin alerts ([`lib/services/courier-order-confirmation.service.ts`](../../lib/services/courier-order-confirmation.service.ts), [`lib/services/wallet-spending.service.ts`](../../lib/services/wallet-spending.service.ts), [`lib/services/orders.service.ts`](../../lib/services/orders.service.ts)) so:
     - `50/50 DEPOSIT_PLUS_COD` messages explicitly state both the online deposit verified (`walletPaidZar` + `providerPaidZar`) and the exact remaining cash due to the driver on delivery (`cashDueZar`) — never claiming "fully paid" or "bank deposit received".
     - `WALLET_ONLY` full parcel settlements emit the same canonical `COURIER_PAYMENT_CONFIRMED` / `COURIER_ACCEPTED` customer intents and `PARCEL_ORDER_PAID` / `PARCEL_ORDER_ACCEPTED` admin alerts (and `cash-on-delivery.service.ts` matches `WALLET_CONSUME:<payment.publicReference>` case-insensitively).
     - `FULL_COD` messages distinguish cash-on-delivery acceptance (`R<cashDueZar> cash due to driver at delivery`) cleanly and reject high-risk cash leaks.

3. **W3 — Complete 33-Event Financial & Operational Owner Alert Coverage (`Sec 3A`):**
   - Extended `ADMIN_ALERT_EVENT_TYPES` in [`lib/settings/admin-alert-recipient-policy.ts`](../../lib/settings/admin-alert-recipient-policy.ts) from `24` to `33` typed events, adding `REFUND_REQUESTED`, `REFUND_COMPLETED`, `WALLET_CREDIT_ISSUED`, `LOYALTY_ADJUSTED`, `REFERRAL_REWARD_AWARDED`, `COUPON_PROMOTION_REDEEMED`, `UNASSIGNED_ORDER_ESCALATED`, `DRIVER_CASH_REMITTANCE_OVERDUE`, and `FINANCE_ANOMALY_ALERT`.
   - Wired atomic `recordAdminAlertIntentInTx` + non-blocking post-commit dispatch across:
     - Refunds & anomalies: [`lib/services/refund-request.service.ts`](../../lib/services/refund-request.service.ts), [`lib/services/refund-wallet-completion.service.ts`](../../lib/services/refund-wallet-completion.service.ts), [`lib/services/refund-provider-execution.service.ts`](../../lib/services/refund-provider-execution.service.ts)
     - Withdrawals & payout anomalies: [`lib/services/withdrawal-payout.service.ts`](../../lib/services/withdrawal-payout.service.ts), [`lib/services/paystack-transfer-execution.service.ts`](../../lib/services/paystack-transfer-execution.service.ts)
     - SuperAdmin wallet grants: [`lib/services/admin-customer-wallet-grant.service.ts`](../../lib/services/admin-customer-wallet-grant.service.ts)
     - Loyalty adjustments, redemptions & post-payment earnings: [`lib/rewards/customer-loyalty-admin.service.ts`](../../lib/rewards/customer-loyalty-admin.service.ts), [`lib/rewards/customer-loyalty-redemption.service.ts`](../../lib/rewards/customer-loyalty-redemption.service.ts), [`lib/rewards/customer-parcel-loyalty.service.ts`](../../lib/rewards/customer-parcel-loyalty.service.ts)
     - Verified first-order referrals: [`lib/rewards/ordinary-referral-award.service.ts`](../../lib/rewards/ordinary-referral-award.service.ts)
     - Paid coupon / promotion redemptions: [`lib/promotions/promotion-redemption.service.ts`](../../lib/promotions/promotion-redemption.service.ts), [`lib/marketplace-checkout/prisma-marketplace-finalization.repository.ts`](../../lib/marketplace-checkout/prisma-marketplace-finalization.repository.ts)
   - Added orphan-intent sweep in `retryPendingAdminAlertDeliveries` and `hasPendingAdminAlertWork` so committed transactional `DOMAIN_EVENT` intents always fan out even after nested commits or worker restarts, without leaking customer PII (`safePayload` / `sanitizeAdminMetadata`).

4. **W4 — Enforceable Daily Driver Cash Remittance SLA & Unassigned Order Escalation (`Sec 4`):**
   - Added nullable `CashOnDelivery.remittanceDueAt` + composite index `@@index([status, remittanceDueAt])` via [`prisma/migrations/20261118020000_driver_cash_remittance_due_at/migration.sql`](../../prisma/migrations/20261118020000_driver_cash_remittance_due_at/migration.sql).
   - [`lib/services/driver-cash-overdue-policy.ts`](../../lib/services/driver-cash-overdue-policy.ts) computes `Africa/Johannesburg` (`UTC+02:00`, no DST) operating-day deadlines (`computeDriverCashRemittanceDueAt`), and `buildOverdueDriverCashWhere` catches both `remittanceDueAt <= now` and legacy `remittanceDueAt: null` rows collected before today's Johannesburg midnight.
   - [`lib/services/driver-cash-capacity.service.ts`](../../lib/services/driver-cash-capacity.service.ts) (`DriverCashRemittanceOverdueError` HTTP `409`) blocks new cash/COD dispatch assignments for any driver holding overdue unverified COD cash while preserving `DIGITAL_ONLY` eligibility; submitting a bank proof alone never clears overdue custody until an independent administrator verifies bank receipt in `verifyDailyCashReconciliation` ([`lib/client-platform/driver-cash.service.ts`](../../lib/client-platform/driver-cash.service.ts)).
   - Updated [`components/forms/DriverCash.tsx`](../../components/forms/DriverCash.tsx) and [`components/admin/DailyCashReconciliation.tsx`](../../components/admin/DailyCashReconciliation.tsx) to surface overdue COD obligations and assignment blocks clearly.
   - Registered two new leased workers in [`lib/processors/processor-registry.ts`](../../lib/processors/processor-registry.ts), [`lib/processors/processor-service.ts`](../../lib/processors/processor-service.ts), [`lib/processors/production-loop.ts`](../../lib/processors/production-loop.ts), and [`scripts/production-processors.ts`](../../scripts/production-processors.ts) (bringing total processors from `30` to `32`: `29` scheduled + `3` delegated composites):
     - `monitor-overdue-driver-cash` ([`lib/services/driver-cash-overdue-sla.service.ts`](../../lib/services/driver-cash-overdue-sla.service.ts), `DRY_RUN` + `APPLY`, 6-hour SAST deduplicated `DRIVER_CASH_REMITTANCE_OVERDUE` alerts, strictly read-only with respect to ledger balances)
