# Production release authorization — implementation in progress

`Engineering Certification` verifies source-level engineering evidence; its success is not permission to charge, refund, pay out, activate commercial policy or promote a release. The separate `Production Release Authorization` workflow is manual and references the `production-release` environment. The repository cannot prove that environment has independent required reviewers configured: the owner must configure and verify those protections outside source control before any use.

The manual entrypoint currently always refuses authorization and records a categorized, redacted refusal. It does not promote a deployment or enable financial controls. This is an intentional temporary limitation, not a complete release-authority integration. `scripts/release-attestation.mjs` implements fixture-tested Ed25519 signature and claim verification, but returns `releaseAuthorized: false`: its caller-supplied trust, deployment and evidence inputs are not independently trustworthy by themselves. No production trust service, current deployment/evidence verifier, revocation source, or durable atomic issuance-ID consumption service has been configured or connected. Do not replace those components with repository JSON, ordinary database settings or freely editable workflow variables.

Before an authorized operator may attempt a release, an independently administered service must supply current trusted and non-revoked reviewer keys, verified identities and roles, current protected deployment and successful exact-SHA CI evidence, evidence-reference digests and approved policy versions; it must atomically prevent replay or revoked/replaced issuances. All four distinct operator, finance, database and security reviewers must be different from the initiator. The attestation binds the repository, production environment and audience, SHA and tree, CI evidence, Vercel and Railway deployment identifiers, scope, policy versions, evidence references/digests, issue/expiry and issuance ID. Expired, missing, forged, mismatched, revoked or partially signed attestations must refuse. Signing keys stay outside the repository, disposable CI and ordinary application database.

**Hosting remains a separate unresolved control.** Existing Vercel and Railway configuration auto-deploys `main`; this source change does not gate or reverse that behavior. Before claiming protected promotion, the hosting owner must review a plan to separate candidate builds from production alias/service promotion, configure independent reviewer protection and rollback, and verify actual deployment parity. Do not silently modify domains, services, credentials, payment flags or current production policies.

For a future authorized change, the owner and independent reviewers should: verify current least-privilege protected-table baseline and queue health; review genuine role/provider/finance/device evidence; confirm the signed scope and exact candidate; verify the protected workflow and trust service; record approvals outside source; and perform only the separately approved promotion. Abort on a mismatched SHA, unhealthy or unknown provider state, missing evidence, replay/revocation, unexpected protected-row change, unreviewed policy or failed health check. Follow the established deployment rollback and financial reconciliation runbooks; never fabricate a settlement to make a release appear successful.
