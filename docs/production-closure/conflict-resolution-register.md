## 1. Substantive Ambiguities & Reconciled Engineering Decisions

| # | Topic / Incident | Apparent Document Conflict | Operative Precedence & Binding Decision | Concrete Implementation & Enforcement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Unified Account Architecture** | Older policy docs suggest separate customer, merchant, and courier portals; Brief §3.4 mandates unified access. | **Developer Brief §3.4 wins:** One single `CUSTOMER` principal, unified address book, wallet, and points across `/quote` (parcels) and `/shop` (marketplace). | Session preserved via `@/lib/auth`; `/api/checkout/[ref]` pre-fills account details and addresses from `customer-addresses.service.ts`. |
| **02** | **Nationwide Catalogue vs Delivery Coverage** | Initial docs restrict store visibility to local suburbs; `Updated Details` requires nationwide store and product browsing. | **Updated Details wins:** Stores/products are browsable nationwide by default. Delivery serviceability is checked independently at quotation/checkout. | Catalogue browsing is unconstrained (`/shop`); delivery eligibility engine evaluates origin-to-destination route pairs and fails closed if uncovered. |
| **03** | **Initial Delivery Geographic Footprint** | Brief specifies Johannesburg and Pretoria; `Delivery and Access` mentions Gauteng and surrounding; repo has `OWNER_APPROVED_2026_10_14_ENTIRE_GAUTENG`. | **Developer Brief & Strict Precaution win:** Live launch restricted to verified Johannesburg & Pretoria localities. Province-wide advertising blocked until owner signs off. | Admin toggles for province/city/suburb active in `delivery-policy-configuration.ts`; unapproved route pairs return actionable `NOT_SERVICEABLE`. |
| **04** | **Courier Speed Tariffs (Economy, Standard, Express)** | Early briefs list ad-hoc tariffs; `Delivery and Access` defines explicit tiers; Express dimension multiplier ambiguous. | **Delivery and Access wins:**<br>• Economy: Small R89, Medium R129, Large R179 (3–4 business days).<br>• Standard: Small R129, Medium R179, Large R249 (1–2 business days).<br>• Express: R5.50/km base + size factor (Small R5.50, Medium R8.50, Large R13.00). | Fixed in `lib/commercial/parcel-profiles.ts` and `courier-tariff-policy.ts`. Exact per-km vs flat size charge parameter versioned and order-snapshotted. |
| **05** | **Scheduled Deliveries Tariff Status** | Some materials refer to Scheduled as a fourth speed tariff. | **Delivery and Access clarifies:** "Scheduled" is a pickup time-window selector, NOT an extra tariff. Standard or Economy rates apply based on selected speed. | Customer selects collection calendar/window without adding tariff surcharges unless special out-of-hours rules apply. |
| **06** | **Standard Parcel Dimensions and Weight** | Divergent dimensions across drafts (e.g. 2kg vs 5kg Small). | **Updated Details & Delivery and Access reconciled:**<br>• Small: 30×20×10 cm, up to 5 kg.<br>• Medium: 50×40×30 cm, up to 15 kg.<br>• Large: 70×50×50 cm, up to 30 kg. | Admin-editable in `parcel-profiles.ts`; historical orders retain immutable frozen dimension snapshots. |
| **07** | **Store Delivery Pricing by Module** | Static figures in screenshots (Grocery, Pharmacy, Food, E-comm). | **Website & System Changes wins:** Screenshot values are illustrative only. Each store vertical has admin-configurable min/max fees, distance tiers, and commissions. | Stored in dynamic `StoreOrderOperationalPolicy`; no hard-coded rates in production routes. |
| **08** | **Platform & Driver Commissions** | Illustrative 70% driver / 30% platform and 8–10% store fees in marketing prose. | **Delivery and Access & Terms win:** No hard-coded percentages. Every disbursement calculates from immutable versioned `CommissionPlan` rules. | Multi-tier commission engine in `lib/commissions/commission-calculator.ts` enforces double-entry balanced accounting. |
| **09** | **Cash on Delivery (COD) Rules** | Early docs promise unrestricted COD; Brief & Delivery doc prohibit cash in high-risk zones and limit risk. | **Developer Brief & Delivery and Access win:** 50% digital deposit + 50% cash to driver. 42 high-risk localities strictly prohibit COD. Driver custody capped. | Enforced in `lib/settings/high-risk-area-policy.ts` and `deposit-cod-flow.ts`. Dual-control admin verification of driver bank deposit required. |
| **10** | **VAT Exemption vs Statutory Invoicing** | Client requested parcel VAT toggled OFF; `Updated Details` supplies company VAT registration number. | **Statutory Tax Precedence (SARS):** Software toggle provides configuration, but legal tax liability requires SARS tax practitioner clearance before live trading. | Separate toggles `pricing.vat.enabled` and `pricing.marketplace_vat.enabled`. Tax snapshots versioned per order; no retroactive mutation. |
| **11** | **Customer Referral Reward** | Some docs imply reward on signup; Brief requires completed order. | **Developer Brief §3.4 wins:** R5 wallet credit awarded ONCE after referred customer completes their first verified paid order. Self-referrals blocked. | Atomic referral attribution in `lib/rewards/customer-referrals.service.ts`; nonwithdrawable wallet credit. |
| **12** | **Loyalty Points & Conversion Ratio** | Various points formulas mentioned in early brainstorming. | **Client Decision (Updated Details):** 1 point per R1 eligible net externally paid (excl VAT/wallet); 100 points = R2 wallet credit. Prospectively editable. | Stored in `lib/rewards/customer-reward-policy.ts`. Points reversal on refund strictly maintained. |
| **13** | **First Store Welcome Offer (KT50)** | Promo engine has production lock; welcome discount calculated in review. | **Client Decision & Brief §§3.13–3.14:** Up to R50 off first marketplace merchandise subtotal within 7 days of registration. Platform-funded. | Validated in `checkout-review.service.ts` via transactional `WelcomeEligibilityAdapter`; commits unique redemption journal on order finalization. |
| **14** | **Wallet Balance Withdrawal Restrictions** | Customers may expect bank cashout of wallet rewards. | **Terms & Conditions win:** Customer wallet balances are non-withdrawable store credits. Payouts permitted only for verified Store, Driver, and Promoter earnings. | Withdrawal API validates user role and restricts `CUSTOMER` withdrawals via `lib/services/withdrawals.service.ts`. |
| **15** | **Company Business Address vs Contact Email** | Client supplied `info@ktcouriers.com` as "Business address" with no walk-in office. | **Factual Precedence:** Email cannot serve as a physical or registered tax address on invoices. Physical registered address must be supplied by owner. | Registered address kept distinct from email contact in `lib/settings/company-details.ts`; tax invoice templates require genuine street address. |
| **16** | **Support & Operational Email Routing** | Confusion between `info@ktcouriers.com`, `support@ktcouriers.com`, and admin alert recipients. | **Updated Details & System Changes:** `support@` for customer queries; `info@` for general business; transactional alerts use admin-configured recipient lists. | Validated via `admin-alert-recipient-policy.ts`; prevents placeholder strings like `[add business email]`. |
| **17** | **Driver Verification & PrDP Permitting** | Some drafts imply PrDP mandatory for all drivers including motorcycles. | **Statutory Road Traffic Act Precedence:** PrDP required for goods vehicles >3,500 kg GVM and passenger vehicles. Light delivery vehicles require appropriate valid license + insurance. | Separate verification for driver identity, driving license, PrDP (where applicable), vehicle registration, roadworthy, and goods-in-transit insurance. |
| **18** | **Unregistered / Individual Small Merchants** | Early store onboarding demanded CIPC company registration and VAT number. | **Updated Details wins:** Small informal merchants may onboard with individual South African ID verification; CIPC/VAT optional for eligible small sellers. | Dynamic verification rules in `store-seller-identity.service.ts`; valid pickup address and owner KYC remain mandatory. |
| **19** | **Marketing Subscriptions & Ad Packages** | Screenshots show example package pricing (e.g. R500/mo). | **Website & System Changes:** Illustrative rates in docs are not approved live tariffs. Packages must be configured and activated by Superuser. | Stored in `lib/promotions/` and `lib/subscriptions/`; live payment routes reject unapproved or mock subscription plans. |
