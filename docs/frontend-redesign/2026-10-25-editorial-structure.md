# Editorial structure and motion pacing — 25 October 2026

## Brief and diagnosis

Keep the approved white palette, Mona typography, curtain/logo entrance, hero wave/zoom, photo orbit, handoff aperture, contained vehicles and destination page curtains. The problem is repetitive composition and boundary motion, not a shortage of effects. Do not change backend, booking, shop/catalog, account, payment or tracking behavior.

The former homepage made six sequential boundary holds feel like its primary visual language. Large text/photo splits repeated, the local gallery lacked a purposeful interaction, Coverage repeated another split, and preparation used another text/image split. Fewer effects alone would not correct this structural sameness.

## Image and interaction roles

All images are existing, locally hosted WebPs, inspected in contact sheets before use. Repository filenames are sometimes misleading; visible content determines alt text. The neighbourhood spread adds produce, handmade bags, fashion and homeware photographs without adding a product feed, prices or claims of catalog availability. No generated images or competitor assets.

The story selectors are real buttons with aria-pressed, a named control group and aria-controls. They do not navigate or submit data. All six images remain mounted; the active pair crossfades without moving the layout. The current caption is announced politely. Focus remains on the selected button. No timers, drag-only gestures or mandatory horizontal scrolling.

Mobile uses in-flow compositions: short opening lines, a main photo plus a readable inset/caption, vertically arranged selectors, a two-image location spread and staggered preparation articles. Existing fixed navigation remains. Reduced motion removes the new crossfades and movement, leaving all content and actions usable. Focus completes new reveal effects immediately.

## Acceptance

- First post-hero statement never uses a takeover or whole-section mask.
- Four homepage takeovers; no blanket section pinning added.
- Page curtain, header/footer, service destinations and operational truth preserved.
- Story buttons change both photos and caption without navigation or layout shift.
- No invented stock, prices, customer quotes, performance metrics or service areas.
- Type/lint, existing public-page contracts, production build, desktop/mobile visual review and live selector checks before completion.
