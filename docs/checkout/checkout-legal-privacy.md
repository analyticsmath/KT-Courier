# Checkout legal and privacy

Review evidence includes seller identity, goods, variants, modifiers, fees, tax
treatment where authoritative, policies and terms acknowledgement. Checkout
snapshots minimise contact/address data, do not add an address to an account by
default, do not use data for marketing, and omit raw coordinates from public
responses/logs.

Seller-of-record evidence is sourced only from an approved effective legal seller
identity during review. It may include public store reference, legal/trading name,
registration reference, authoritative VAT status/number, country, version, terms
and invoice classification. It excludes bank details, owner identity, private
contacts, internal moderation and mutable dashboard/performance data. Incomplete
authority fails review closed and is not inferred from storefront content. This is
not final legal approval.
