# Physical device acceptance

Automated Chromium viewports do not prove that a driver can use a physical phone, camera or GPS service. No physical device, real camera permission, GPS accuracy or field delivery flow was exercised in this work.

An operations/QA owner must identify the tested device and OS, exact driver journey, permission state, network conditions, evidence capture and result. This is a required human acceptance item for driver/POD and media launch; it remains open.
