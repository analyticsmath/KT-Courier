# KT Couriers — Refund and Cancellation Policy

> **COUNSEL-REVIEW DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED.** Proposed editorial and legal rewrite. The live policy remains the immutable, database-managed publication, and this draft does not amend any customer agreement. Dates and approval status must be determined through the established publication workflow.

## 1. Our approach

We want customers to know how to raise a concern, how a cancellation or refund request is assessed and what happens after a decision has been made.

Because KT Couriers connects customers with independent stores, vendors and delivery partners, the party responsible for a problem and the available remedy may differ according to the transaction. This policy covers qualifying marketplace orders, courier bookings, delivery charges, payment errors and related matters.

**Your statutory rights come first.** Nothing in this policy excludes or restricts rights and remedies available under applicable South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 where applicable.

## 2. When to contact us

Please report a concern as soon as reasonably possible if an order or delivery is missing, incorrect, damaged, unsafe, substantially different from its description, or otherwise not supplied as agreed. You should also contact us if you notice a duplicate, incorrect or unauthorised charge.

An investigation may be required to identify whether the issue concerns the vendor's product, preparation or fulfilment; KT Couriers' delivery service; the delivery partner; the payment processor; or information supplied by the sender or recipient.

The outcome may be a refund, replacement, redelivery, correction, partial refund, lawful store credit or another appropriate remedy depending on the circumstances and applicable law.

## 3. Cancelling before preparation or dispatch

If you wish to cancel an order or courier booking before it is prepared, collected or dispatched, contact us promptly and provide the relevant reference.

A cancellation may qualify for a refund of the amount paid where the goods or service have not been supplied and any applicable cancellation conditions have been met. Any permitted deduction for costs already incurred must be lawful and properly justified.

Do not assume that submitting a cancellation request alone stops the fulfilment or dispatch process. Confirmation will depend on the stage of the order and the ability of the relevant parties to intervene.

## 4. Cancelling after preparation, collection or dispatch

Once a vendor has begun preparing made-to-order goods, or a courier has collected or commenced delivery, cancellation may no longer be practicable. A lawful charge may apply for services already performed or costs reasonably incurred, subject to the circumstances and applicable rights.

This is particularly relevant to food, prepared groceries, personalised products, urgent deliveries and other time-sensitive services. Where the law provides a cancellation right or remedy despite the preparation or dispatch stage, that right remains unaffected.

## 5. Incorrect, damaged, defective or missing goods

If goods are incorrect, defective, damaged, incomplete or materially different from what you ordered, contact support with the order reference and a description of the problem.

To help investigate, we may reasonably request photographs of the product and packaging, relevant correspondence, proof-of-delivery information or other evidence. We will consider whether the issue arose during supply, packing or transport and consult the relevant vendor or delivery partner as appropriate.

Any repair, replacement, refund or other remedy will be determined according to the nature of the issue, the relevant contract and mandatory consumer law. Vendor policies may apply to the extent that they do not unlawfully reduce your rights.

## 6. Food, groceries and perishable goods

Food and other perishable products should be checked promptly on receipt. Please report incorrect, spoiled, unsafe, missing or significantly damaged items as soon as reasonably possible; immediate reporting may assist with an investigation.

Perishability, storage conditions and handling after delivery may be relevant to what happened. They do not justify denying a remedy that is required under applicable law. Where appropriate, an affected item may be replaced, refunded or otherwise remedied.

## 7. Pharmacy, healthcare and regulated products

Pharmacy, health and wellness products may be subject to additional legal, safety and return requirements. Some supplied or opened products may not be suitable for return, but restrictions do not cancel mandatory remedies for defective, incorrect or unsafe goods.

Contact KT Couriers and the relevant licensed pharmacy or vendor without delay if you receive an incorrect or problematic item. The relevant healthcare business may need to assess a regulated product or prescription issue in accordance with applicable requirements.

## 8. General marketplace and retail purchases

A customer's rights concerning retail goods depend on the product, its condition, the nature of the complaint, the vendor's lawful return arrangements and applicable consumer legislation.

KT Couriers may coordinate the complaint process or facilitate communication with the responsible vendor. The vendor remains responsible for obligations applicable to goods it has sold, and KT Couriers remains responsible for its own services and any legal obligations it has assumed.

## 9. Courier-service issues and non-delivery

A courier fee may qualify for refund or adjustment where the paid-for service was not provided, a qualifying delivery failure was attributable to KT Couriers, the fee was incorrectly charged or another contractual or statutory entitlement applies.

Non-delivery caused by an inaccurate address, an inaccessible premises, an unavailable recipient, prohibited or improperly packaged goods, or missing required instructions may instead result in an attempted-delivery charge or an offer of redelivery where permitted.

Every case should be considered on its facts. No automatic rule in this policy removes a mandatory remedy where the provider is legally responsible for the failure.

## 10. Delays and estimated times

Delivery windows and estimated times can be affected by traffic, severe weather, vendor preparation, address accuracy, security conditions, order volumes and operational disruption. A change to an estimate does not necessarily establish an entitlement to a refund.

Where a material delay constitutes a breach of the agreed service, or where South African law provides a remedy, the relevant rights will be considered. If the delivery has not occurred, contact us with the booking reference so that the circumstances can be investigated.

## 11. Delivery charges and service fees

The product purchase price and any separate delivery or service charge may be assessed independently. A refunded product does not automatically mean the delivery service was improperly performed, just as a successful delivery does not excuse supplying defective goods.

Whether a delivery fee is refunded depends on which service failed, who was responsible, the cancellation stage, any lawful deductions and the applicable legal remedy.

## 12. Subscriptions, promotions and vouchers

Where a qualifying subscription service is offered, cancellation of future billing and treatment of services already supplied will depend on the disclosed subscription terms and applicable law.

Promotional discounts, vouchers and store credits may be governed by their own lawful conditions. Where an order is refunded, promotional benefits will be treated in accordance with the qualifying promotion. A promotional benefit generally does not represent a separate cash amount unless expressly stated or legally required.

If you suspect unauthorised use of a voucher, account or payment instrument, contact us promptly.

## 13. Duplicate, incorrect or unauthorised payments

If you believe the same transaction was charged twice, the amount was wrong, a failed order was charged or a payment was not authorised, email support with the relevant reference, date and amount.

We will investigate the transaction and, where appropriate, work with the payment provider or responsible party to correct it. Do not send complete card numbers, passwords or verification codes in your support email.

## 14. How to request a refund

Email **support@ktcouriers.com** or use the contact form and include:

- Your full name and a reliable contact method.
- The order, delivery or transaction reference.
- The date of the transaction and the item or service affected.
- A clear explanation of the requested remedy and what happened.
- Photographs or other relevant evidence where reasonably available.

Missing evidence will be considered in context. We may ask follow-up questions where reasonably necessary and will explain the outcome as required by law.

## 15. Assessment and decision

We may review order information, delivery events, vendor explanations, payment records and the evidence provided. Some cases require cooperation from a store, delivery partner, bank or payment service provider.

Submitting a request is not a guarantee that a discretionary claim will be approved. Equally, a statutory entitlement is not defeated merely because the Platform requires an investigation or internal approval.

Where the remedy is a refund, the amount should reflect the applicable entitlement and any lawful deduction or apportionment.

## 16. How and when refunds are processed

Where a refund is approved, KT Couriers will generally submit it for processing **within seven business days after approval**. Submission to the payment provider and the later appearance of funds in your account are separate events.

Processing times may depend on the payment method, provider, bank, public holidays and the relevant verification or settlement arrangements. We will generally seek to return the funds to the original payment method. If that is not available, an alternative may require identity or ownership verification.

A promised timeframe will not override any stricter obligation imposed by applicable law.

## 17. Restricted returns and misuse

Certain perishable, personalised, hygiene, regulated or fully performed services may have lawful return restrictions. They must be applied with regard to the reason for the claim and mandatory consumer protections.

We may investigate suspected fraudulent, duplicate or abusive claims and take proportionate action where permitted. A good-faith complaint or the lawful exercise of consumer rights must not be treated as abuse merely because it is disputed.

KT Couriers may update this policy when services or legal requirements change. Each approved version must be published through the controlled legal-document workflow with its own effective date and, where necessary, an appropriate customer notice.
