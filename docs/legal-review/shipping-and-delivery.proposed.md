# KT Couriers — Shipping and Delivery Policy

> **COUNSEL-REVIEW DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED.** Proposed public policy wording based on the supplied client documents and operating rules. It must be reviewed against the current live quote engine, coverage configuration and contracts before publication as a new immutable legal-document version.

## 1. What this policy covers

KT Couriers provides access to marketplace and courier-delivery services connecting customers, participating stores and delivery partners. This policy explains the information required for a delivery, available service categories, route and service-area checks, packaging and handover responsibilities, tracking, delivery exceptions and how problems are handled.

A delivery may involve a seller, sender, recipient, delivery partner and KT Couriers. Their respective obligations depend on the specific service and transaction. This policy does not limit mandatory South African consumer rights.

## 2. Services and availability

Depending on operational capacity and the service selected, delivery requests may relate to parcels, documents, marketplace purchases, food and groceries, eligible pharmacy products, online retail orders, business collections, scheduled deliveries, express services or more specialised logistics.

The presence of a service category on the website does not mean that it is operationally available for every route, commodity or requested date. Availability is confirmed through the relevant quote, booking, vendor or support process.

Some services may require additional review, specialised capacity or conditions before acceptance. No request is confirmed merely because a category is displayed.

## 3. Current operational coverage

KT Couriers' current courier operations primarily serve Gauteng, including Johannesburg, Pretoria and surrounding areas where the relevant service has been activated.

The Platform is designed to support wider participation and future expansion across South Africa. A store being visible to customers nationally is different from KT Couriers being able to deliver a particular order to a particular address.

Courier service and province or area availability are configured independently. Express or same-day service may be available only in activated areas with confirmed capacity. Locations not yet enabled should not be represented as available for an actual delivery.

## 4. Getting a quotation

You can request a courier quotation without opening an account. We may require the pickup address, delivery address, parcel size, weight where applicable, requested service, and practical handling information necessary to assess the request.

The Platform may calculate a price based on approved tariff rules, route information, size, distance, service level, operational area and other legitimately configured factors. The quote and the confirmed booking details — rather than a fixed price table printed in this policy — determine the applicable amount.

Registration, verification and payment may be required to proceed from a quotation to a protected booking. We may request clarification or refuse a shipment where the description or route cannot be verified.

## 5. Economy, Standard and Express

Available service levels may include:

- **Economy:** a lower-priority option with an indicative target turnaround of three to four business days, subject to availability and the confirmed quote.
- **Standard:** a service level with an indicative target turnaround of one to two business days where operationally supported, subject to the confirmed quote.
- **Express or same-day:** an accelerated service only where the route, capacity and cut-off arrangements permit, with its actual timing and price confirmed for the request.

These are service descriptions and indicative targets, not unconditional delivery guarantees. The final tariff, availability, cut-off and timing applicable to a particular order must be shown at confirmation.

**Scheduled delivery is not a separate delivery-speed category.** It refers to choosing a future available collection or delivery date or time window. A scheduled booking may use Standard or another eligible service level, with the applicable confirmed tariff for that underlying service.

## 6. Marketplace and vendor orders

Participating vendors are generally responsible for keeping product listings accurate, accepting and preparing orders, packaging goods appropriately and making them ready for collection within the arrangements confirmed.

Fulfilment timing may therefore depend on vendor preparation as well as courier availability. Where a store has an agreed delivery tariff or special arrangement, the price presented for the relevant order should reflect its applicable configuration.

Product prices, vendor-specific shipping conditions, delivery charges and any other separately disclosed fees must be understandable before a customer confirms a transaction.

## 7. Packaging and item information

Senders and vendors must package articles suitably for their nature, weight, fragility and expected handling. They should provide an accurate description, appropriate labels and clear special-handling instructions where necessary.

Fragile, valuable, temperature-sensitive or regulated items may need prior approval or packaging, protection and insurance arrangements beyond standard courier handling. Acceptance of a package does not create insurance cover or a compensation guarantee unless an applicable arrangement expressly provides it.

The sender must not tender unsafe, leaking, inadequately secured or unlawful goods.

## 8. Prohibited and restricted items

Do not request transport of contraband, hazardous substances, illegal products or goods prohibited under applicable law or service rules. Particular medicines, chemicals, flammable articles, batteries, fragile or high-value products may be subject to additional restrictions and confirmation requirements.

We may request further information, refuse collection or suspend a booking when reasonable safety, legal or operational concerns arise. An applicable prohibited-goods schedule or specialist-service agreement may impose further conditions.

## 9. Collection and address responsibilities

Customers and senders should provide complete collection and delivery addresses, unit numbers, entry instructions, working contact details, accurate recipient information and any circumstances affecting safe access.

The sender or authorised representative should make the agreed item available for collection. The recipient or authorised alternative should be available to complete the handover where required.

Incorrect information, lack of access, recipient unavailability, prohibited goods or inadequate packaging may cause a failed attempt, an additional lawful charge, a rescheduled delivery or a return to sender depending on the circumstances.

## 10. Order and delivery status

The Platform may show recorded order, collection, in-transit or delivery milestones, depending on the service. A status update is not necessarily a continuous location feed or a guarantee of a specific arrival minute.

Estimated times can change with vendor readiness, traffic, weather, access restrictions, network demand and incidents outside normal control. We will use reasonable efforts to communicate relevant changes where practicable.

For assistance with an active order, use the appropriate account page or contact support with the reference.

## 11. Delivery verification and proof

A delivery may require an authorised recipient, signature, photograph, recorded status, delivery code or other suitable proof, depending on the operational workflow and legal requirements.

Where a one-time delivery code is used, it must be given only in connection with the correct authorised handover. Never disclose account login credentials or unrelated security codes to a courier.

A delivery record or proof-of-delivery event may be reviewed if the handover is later disputed. A recorded event does not prevent investigation where the customer reasonably disputes what occurred.

## 12. Failed delivery and redelivery

When a delivery cannot be completed, the reason may be recorded and the customer, recipient or vendor contacted for further instructions.

Possible next steps include another delivery attempt, confirmation of corrected details, a return to sender, referral to the responsible vendor or another lawful resolution.

Any additional fee must arise from the relevant agreement or a lawful basis and should be disclosed where applicable. The responsibility for a failed attempt will be assessed on the facts.

## 13. Delivery delays and interruptions

Delivery times may be affected by weather, traffic, road closures, security issues, vendor preparation, high demand, incomplete addresses, recipient availability or technical outages.

An estimate is not a guarantee unless the particular service expressly provides a guaranteed commitment. If a significant delay means a service has not been supplied as agreed, contractual and statutory remedies will be considered under the applicable Refund and Cancellation Policy.

## 14. Missing, damaged or incorrect deliveries

Contact **support@ktcouriers.com** as soon as reasonably possible if an item is missing, damaged, incomplete or delivered incorrectly.

Provide the order reference, a description of what occurred and any useful photographs or evidence. We may review collection and custody events, the packaging, vendor records, recipient confirmation, payment records and delivery-partner information as appropriate.

Remedies or compensation depend on who was responsible, the item and service terms, any lawful declared-value or insurance arrangement and applicable law. We do not state an unverified universal liability or insurance limit in this draft.

## 15. Specialist, long-distance and future services

Freight, furniture moves, removals, overnight requests, high-value shipments and international transport may require separate quotations, agreements, insurance, customs or regulatory checks.

A future service category does not represent an active commitment until KT Couriers has confirmed the relevant capacity and service conditions. Cross-border and international transport remains subject to applicable law and the required approvals.

## 16. Changes, policies and contact

Operational areas, services, tariffs and capacity may change. The active booking process should provide the current offer, and any material change to a confirmed booking should be handled under its terms and applicable law.

This policy should be read with the Terms and Conditions, Refund and Cancellation Policy, Privacy Policy and any applicable vendor or specialist-service terms. Rights conferred by South African law remain unaffected.

For delivery questions, email **support@ktcouriers.com**. For general business matters, email **info@ktcouriers.com**. KT Couriers does not currently operate a public walk-in office.
