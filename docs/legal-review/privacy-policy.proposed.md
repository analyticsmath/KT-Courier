## 1. Our commitment to your privacy

KT COURIERS (PTY) LTD ("KT Couriers", "we", "us" or "our") respects the personal information entrusted to it by customers, store owners, employees, applicants, delivery partners, business contacts and people visiting the Platform.

This Privacy Policy explains the personal information we may collect, how and why we use it, when we share it, the measures used to protect it and the choices and rights available to you. It applies when you visit our website, request a quote, place or receive an order, operate a store, join the delivery network or contact our team.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable South African law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") where applicable. KT Couriers may act as the responsible party for processing carried out for its own business purposes. Participating vendors, payment providers and other organisations may also act independently as responsible parties for processing under their control.

## 2. The information we handle

The information collected depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

- **Contact and identity information:** name, email address, phone number, date of birth or identity details where reasonably necessary for verification.
- **Account and transaction information:** account identifiers, registration details, order references, invoices, purchases, service history, customer preferences and support communications.
- **Pickup and delivery information:** collection and destination addresses, recipient contact information, delivery instructions, shipment descriptions, relevant location data and delivery status records.
- **Store and business information:** trading name, business contacts, registration details, permitted tax and payment information, product listings, inventory or catalogue information and required verification records.
- **Driver and applicant information:** identity and licence evidence, vehicle documents, relevant insurance or service-area records, professional or onboarding information and operational events associated with deliveries.
- **Payment and fraud-prevention information:** transaction references, payment state, amounts, risk signals and information needed for settlement, verification, disputes or fraud review. Card-payment details may be processed by authorised payment providers rather than stored directly by KT Couriers.
- **Technical and usage information:** device and browser characteristics, access logs, necessary cookies, security events and interactions with the Platform where lawfully collected.

We will not require personal information unrelated to a stated, lawful purpose merely as a condition of access to an unrelated service.

## 3. How we collect information

We may obtain information directly from you when you register, fill in a form, place an order, apply to join a network, contact support or update your profile.

We may also obtain it from the vendor or delivery partner involved in a transaction, an authorised business representative, payment and identity-verification providers, technical systems used to operate the Platform or other lawful sources. When obtaining information from someone other than you, we will observe applicable transparency obligations and exceptions.

## 4. Why we process personal information

We may process information for purposes reasonably connected with:

1. Providing quotations, receiving orders, arranging collections and deliveries, processing eligible payments and maintaining transaction records.
2. Registering and administering accounts, vendor stores, driver onboarding and approved business arrangements.
3. Verifying identity, service eligibility and lawful handling requirements, where justified.
4. Sending essential operational notices such as account verification messages, changes to delivery status and responses to service requests.
5. Preventing and investigating fraud, misuse, security incidents and unlawful activity.
6. Resolving support enquiries, delivery incidents, refunds, charge disputes and complaints.
7. Meeting legal, accounting, tax, audit, recordkeeping and regulatory obligations.
8. Improving reliability, accessibility and performance using information handled in accordance with applicable law.
9. Providing marketing communications where lawful and subject to applicable opt-out or consent requirements.

Our lawful basis for processing will depend on the activity and the requirements of POPIA and other applicable legislation. Where consent is required, the relevant request should be specific and voluntary, and withdrawal should be available as provided by law. We do not rely on blanket consent to justify every type of processing.

## 5. Deliveries and location information

Delivery fulfilment may require the collection point, destination, contact details, status events and information reasonably necessary to confirm a collection or handover.

Where location information is used, access should be limited to legitimate operational purposes. A map, ETA or delivery-status view may be approximate and does not necessarily provide continuous location tracking of a driver.

Proof-of-delivery records or delivery verification codes, where used, should be processed only for their intended confirmation and dispute-resolution purposes and protected against unnecessary disclosure.

## 6. When information may be shared

We may share the minimum information reasonably necessary with:

- Vendors responsible for preparing or supplying the goods purchased.
- Delivery partners assigned to complete the relevant collection or delivery.
- Payment, banking, settlement and fraud-prevention providers involved in authorised transactions.
- Infrastructure, communications, hosting, storage, analytics or technical service providers operating under appropriate arrangements.
- Professional advisers, auditors or insurers to the extent reasonably required and legally permitted.
- Regulators, law-enforcement bodies or other persons where disclosure is required or permitted by law.
- Other parties where you have lawfully authorised the disclosure.

Service providers acting as operators should be subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. An independent vendor or third party may have its own privacy policy governing processing for purposes beyond those KT Couriers controls.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

## 7. Security and access

We use organisational and technical measures appropriate to the nature and sensitivity of the information to reduce risks of loss, unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. Measures may include access controls, restricted privileges, secure transmission, verification procedures, monitoring and appropriate supplier safeguards.

No information system can guarantee absolute security. Where an incident triggers notification or other obligations under POPIA or applicable law, we will follow those requirements.

You should safeguard your password, avoid sharing account or delivery verification codes in suspicious circumstances, and report suspected misuse promptly.

## 8. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information only for the period required or permitted for the relevant lawful purpose, including delivery support, contractual obligations, legitimate disputes, legal compliance, financial records and applicable retention duties.

Different record types may require different periods. Once information is no longer required, we will delete, de-identify or securely retain it only where there is a lawful reason to do so.

The final approved retention disclosure must be reconciled with the platform's actual retention schedules, legal holds, processor arrangements and backups. This draft does not claim an unverified number of retention days.

## 9. International services and third parties

Some infrastructure or service providers may process or store information outside South Africa. Where cross-border transfers occur, KT Couriers must ensure that the transfer satisfies applicable POPIA requirements and is subject to suitable protections.

The final published notice must accurately identify the categories of overseas transfers and relevant safeguards based on the actual provider inventory. Links to third-party sites are governed by the relevant third party's own practices; please review their notices where appropriate.

## 10. Marketing, cookies and your choices

We may contact you with relevant service messages necessary for your transactions. Marketing messages are different and are handled in accordance with applicable electronic-communications and privacy rules.

Where required, you may withdraw consent or object to direct marketing through the available preference or unsubscribe mechanism, or by contacting us.

The Platform may use necessary cookies and comparable browser storage for account security, session handling, functionality and service delivery. Any analytics, advertising or other non-essential technologies require accurate disclosure and any applicable choice mechanism. A separate cookie notice may provide further detail once formally reviewed and published.

## 11. Accuracy and your rights

Subject to applicable requirements and exceptions, you may ask us to:

- Confirm whether we hold personal information about you and request access to it.
- Correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete, misleading or outdated.
- Delete or destroy information that we are no longer entitled to retain, where the law permits.
- Object to certain processing or direct marketing, or withdraw consent where consent is relied upon.
- Raise a complaint about the way your information is handled.

We may need to verify your identity before providing access or carrying out a request, and may lawfully refuse or limit a request where an exemption applies. We will explain the relevant outcome as required by law. You may have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

## 12. Children and regulated information

The Platform is not designed as a general service for children acting independently where legal capacity or age restrictions apply. Certain transactions, particularly involving medicines or regulated goods, may be subject to additional restrictions and safeguards.

The handling of children's information, special personal information or regulated records must comply with any specific lawful conditions that apply.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when services, technology, processing practices or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel where required.

Each formally published version must retain its own effective date and publication record. A user acknowledgement should remain linked to the version presented when it was given, and an updated notice must not be retroactively substituted for that record.

## 14. Privacy requests and contact

For questions, access requests, corrections, objections, complaints or other privacy matters, email **support@ktcouriers.com** with "Privacy Request" in the subject where possible.

Business enquiries may be directed to **info@ktcouriers.com**. KT Couriers operates online and does not currently have a public walk-in office. Information Officer details and any legally required physical address or PAIA disclosures must be confirmed and approved for the final publication.
