# R10 privacy data inventory

Status: repository-derived engineering inventory, not a public Privacy Notice and not a legal-basis, retention, or cross-border-processing decision.

| Category | Source route or implementation | Field categories observed | User-facing purpose | Technical purpose / recipient system | Retention / legal basis / international authority | Public-policy status and unresolved input |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer account registration | `/signup`, `app/(auth)/signup/SignupForm.tsx` | Name, email, phone, password | Create a customer account | Authentication and account records | Not established in an approved public notice | Privacy Notice must define the applicable decisions. |
| Store account registration | `/signup?role=store`, `SignupForm.tsx` | Store name, contact name, email, phone, business address, password | Create a store account | Authentication and account records | Not established | Privacy Notice and business-agreement scope required. |
| Authentication and sessions | Auth API routes, `lib/auth/session.ts` | Authentication credentials, OTP evidence, session record | Sign in and secure account access | Session service; opaque session cookie | Session duration exists in code, but public retention/legal wording is not approved | Do not publish token values or treat code as a completed legal disclosure. |
| Contact enquiries | `/contact`, `components/forms/ContactForm.tsx` | Name, email, optional phone, enquiry type, message | Reply to the submitted enquiry | Contact API and application records | Not established | Privacy Notice needs a reviewed description and contact authority. |
| Delivery requests and account orders | Authenticated request/order routes, `components/forms/DeliveryRequestForm.tsx` | Pickup/dropoff contacts, addresses, parcel description/count, schedule and notes | Coordinate a delivery request | Order and operational records | Not established | Delivery/operational agreement and Privacy Notice input required. |
| Coverage and route estimate | Account delivery flow, maps service | Address and route-estimate inputs | Assess request suitability | Maps/route-estimate service when configured | Provider, transfer, and retention authority not established | Do not state Google Maps processing terms without approved source. |
| Marketplace cart and checkout | Locked `/shop`, `/cart`, `/checkout/*`, marketplace APIs | Cart selections, checkout contact/delivery information, payment references | Marketplace operations when enabled | Marketplace and payment services | Not established; public marketplace remains locked | Marketplace terms, seller identity, payment and refund/cancellation decisions remain separate blockers. |
| Recruitment and applicant activity | `/careers`, `/applicant/*`, recruitment APIs | Applicant profile, application answers, interview/check data, uploaded documents | Recruitment application and review | Recruitment application records | Existing internal retention/privacy material is not an approved public Privacy Notice | Approved recruitment notice, retention, and rights route required before publication. |
| Developer applications and webhooks | `/developers`, protected developer routes | Application owner/purpose, credentials and webhook delivery evidence | Govern server-to-server access | Developer application and webhook services | Existing engineering terms boundary is not public legal approval | Developer agreement and privacy decision required. |
| Store, driver and promoter participation | `/join` and protected participation workflows | Role-specific application and operational data where a workflow is enabled | Participation lifecycle | Role-specific operational records | Not established | Do not turn public participation copy into a contract. |
| Notifications and service communication | Auth/order/notification services | Contact endpoints and delivery/status communication records | Account and operational updates | Notification provider/service | Not established | Recipient/processor/retention wording requires authority. |
| Security and audit records | Security, authentication, payment, and audit services | Security event and bounded audit evidence | Secure service operation and investigation | Application security records | Not established | Do not expose security-sensitive schema or retention detail publicly. |
| Cookies and device data | `lib/auth/session.ts`, marketplace token policy | Opaque first-party cookie records; no values listed | Session and currently locked guest-cart capability | Browser cookie storage | See `docs/legal/cookie-storage-inventory.md` | Cookie Notice needs approval; no consent model has been invented. |

The inventory deliberately omits raw tokens, credential material, payment-card data, private identifiers, security-event payloads, and document contents.
